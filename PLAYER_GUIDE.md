## What is Starry Trees?

Starry Trees replaces the normal Minecraft world with a completely unique dimension filled with floating spherical planetoids. Instead of vast landscapes, you'll explore thousands of spheres connected by wooden bridges, each containing different resources, biomes, and challenges.

### Key Features

- **Three Custom Dimensions**: Starry Sky (Overworld), Scary Sky (Nether), and Scarcy Sky (End)
- **315+ Unique Sphere Types**: From simple stone spheres to complex dungeons and treasure vaults
- **Connected World**: Wooden bridges link spheres in tree-like patterns, making exploration an adventure
- **Story Mode**: Discover lore by finding story blocks along your journey
- **Challenging Survival**: No infinite ground - manage your resources carefully!

## Getting Started

### First Steps

When you first spawn in the Starry Sky dimension, you'll find yourself on a **wooden spawn sphere** at coordinates **X:16, Y:90, Z:16**. This sphere is always present and serves as your starting point.

**Important**: Don't fall! There's void below, so always watch your step.

### Your First Minutes

1. **Gather Wood**: The spawn sphere is made of oak wood. Chop some logs immediately.
2. **Make Tools**: Craft a wooden pickaxe, axe, and sword right away.
3. **Look Around**: Spot nearby spheres and the wooden bridges connecting them.
4. **Explore Carefully**: Walk across bridges to reach other spheres.
5. **Find Stone**: Look for stone spheres to upgrade your tools.

### Early Game Checklist

- [ ] Gather 20+ oak logs from spawn sphere
- [ ] Craft basic tools (pickaxe, axe, sword, shovel)
- [ ] Find a stone sphere and mine 30+ cobblestone
- [ ] Upgrade to stone tools
- [ ] Find a dirt/grass sphere to start farming
- [ ] Find a water sphere for infinite water
- [ ] Build a safe base on a large sphere
- [ ] Find food sources (animals on grass spheres, crops)

## Dimensions

### Starry Sky (Overworld Equivalent)

**Portal**: Build a frame from **Packed Ice** blocks (default, configurable) and light it with flint and steel.

**Biome**: "Starry Sky"

**Characteristics**:
- Peaceful atmosphere
- Day/night cycle
- Normal mob spawns based on sphere type
- Most diverse sphere selection

**Starting Strategy**:
- Stay here until you have iron armor and good food supply
- Collect resources from various sphere types
- Build a main base
- Locate stronghold spheres for End portal access

### Scary Sky (Nether Equivalent)

**Portal**: Standard nether portal (obsidian frame) within Starry Sky dimension.

**Biome**: "Scary Sky"

**Characteristics**:
- Reddish atmosphere
- No day/night cycle
- Nether mobs (piglins, blazes, ghasts)
- Nether-specific resources (quartz, gold, ancient debris)

**Dangers**:
- Ghasts can destroy bridges!
- Lava spheres are common
- Hostile mobs everywhere

**Valuable Resources**:
- Ancient Debris spheres (incredibly rare)
- Nether Gold Ore
- Nether Quartz
- Crimson/Warped Wood
- Nether Fortress structures (in special spheres)

### Scarcy Sky (End Equivalent)

**Portal**: Find a stronghold sphere in Starry Sky, locate the End portal, and activate with Eyes of Ender.

**Biome**: "Scarcy Sky"

**Characteristics**:
- Yellowish void
- Endermen everywhere
- End-specific resources
- End City structures (in special spheres)

**Unique Features**:
- End Gateway spheres that teleport you (rare)
- End City spheres with elytra and shulker boxes
- Dragon fight? (Depends on implementation)

## Sphere Types & Finding Resources

Spheres are categorized by type. Learning to recognize them is key to efficient resource gathering!

### Essential Spheres (Common)

#### Stone Sphere
- **Appearance**: Gray, made entirely of stone
- **Resources**: Stone for building and tools
- **Rarity**: Very common
- **Tips**: Your first target after spawn

#### Grass Sphere
- **Appearance**: Green top (grass), brown interior (dirt)
- **Resources**: Dirt, grass, occasional flowers
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Tips**: Animals spawn here - bring wheat seeds to breed animals!

#### Cobblestone Sphere
- **Appearance**: Gray, rough texture
- **Resources**: Cobblestone
- **Rarity**: Common

#### Sand Sphere
- **Appearance**: Tan/beige
- **Resources**: Sand, occasional sandstone core
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Tips**: Good for glass making and concrete

#### Gravel Sphere
- **Appearance**: Gray-brown, pebbly
- **Resources**: Gravel, occasional flint drops
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Tips**: Mine for flint (flint and steel!)

### Wood Spheres (Common)

All wood spheres follow similar pattern: log core, leaf shell

- **Oak Wood**: Brown logs, green leaves
- **Birch Wood**: White logs, light green leaves
- **Spruce Wood**: Dark logs, dark green leaves
- **Jungle Wood**: Orange-brown logs, bushy leaves
- **Acacia Wood**: Orange logs, small leaves
- **Dark Oak Wood**: Very dark logs, thick leaves
- **Azalea Wood**: Special with azalea trees

**Resources**: Logs, leaves, occasional saplings/apples  
**Rarity**: Common  
**Tips**: Sustainable wood source if you don't mine the whole sphere!

### Ore Spheres (Uncommon to Rare)

#### Common Ores
- **Coal Sphere**: Black with coal ore
- **Copper Sphere**: Orange-brown with copper ore
- **Iron Sphere**: Tan with iron ore

#### Uncommon Ores
- **Lapis Sphere**: Blue with lapis ore
- **Redstone Sphere**: Red-tinted with redstone ore (glows!)
- **Gold Sphere**: Yellow-ish with gold ore

#### Rare Ores
- **Diamond Sphere**: Light blue-gray with diamond ore - **VERY RARE**
- **Emerald Sphere**: Green-tinted with emerald ore - **VERY RARE**

**Tips**: 
- Ore spheres are smaller than basic spheres
- Mine the entire sphere - they're made almost entirely of ore!
- Diamond spheres are your endgame resource farm

### Fluid Spheres (Uncommon)

#### Water Sphere
- **Appearance**: Blue, made of water
- **Resources**: Infinite water source
- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Tips**: Bring buckets! Build a base nearby for easy water access

#### Lava Sphere
- **Appearance**: Orange, bubbling lava
- **Resources**: Lava for smelting/nether portal
- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Dangers**: Don't fall in! Fire resistance recommended
- **Tips**: Approach from above, use buckets carefully

### Ice Spheres (Uncommon)

- **Ice Sphere**: Made of regular ice
- **Packed Ice Sphere**: Made of packed ice (needed for portal!)
- **Blue Ice Sphere**: Made of blue ice (super rare)

**Tips**: Silk touch pickaxe preserves ice blocks

### Decorative Spheres (Uncommon)

#### Glass Sphere
- **Appearance**: Transparent
- **Resources**: Glass blocks
- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Tips**: Great for building! Mine with any tool

#### Rainbow Spheres
Multiple types with colorful blocks:
- **Rainbow Wool**: All colored wool
- **Rainbow Concrete**: All concrete colors
- **Rainbow Glass**: All stained glass colors
- **Rainbow Terracotta**: All terracotta colors

**Rarity**: Rare  
**Tips**: Perfect for decorative building!

#### Other Decorative
- **Obsidian Sphere**: Black, very slow to mine
- **Prismarine Sphere**: Ocean-themed blocks
- **Moss Sphere**: Covered in moss
- **Amethyst Sphere**: Purple crystals (rare!)

### Special Spheres (Rare)

#### Cave Sphere
- **Appearance**: Large, hollow with caves inside
- **Resources**: Stone, ores in cave walls, occasional mob spawners
- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Tips**: Bring torches! Like exploring a mini cave system

#### Dungeon Sphere

- **Resources**: Mob spawner, loot chests

## Bridges & Navigation

### Bridge Basics

- **Material**: Wooden planks (oak by default)
- **Structure**: Automatically generated to connect spheres
- **Height**: Gradually changes to match sphere heights
- **Width**: Usually 3-5 blocks wide

### Bridge Safety

**Dangers**:
- Falling off edges (always fatal to void)
- Mob spawning on bridges at night
- Ghasts destroying bridges in Scary Sky
- Getting lost in complex bridge networks

**Safety Tips**:
1. **Add Railings**: Place fences or walls along bridge edges
2. **Light It Up**: Place torches every 8 blocks to prevent mob spawns
3. **Mark Your Path**: Use signs or colored blocks to mark routes
4. **Bring Building Blocks**: Always carry a stack of blocks to build emergency bridges

### Navigation Strategies

#### Marking Systems

**Color Coding**:
- Red wool: Danger (dead end, dangerous sphere)
- Green wool: Important resources
- Blue wool: Path back to base
- Yellow wool: Unexplored paths

**Sign Posts**:
Place signs at intersections:
```
← Base (300m)
→ Diamond Sphere
↑ Water Source
```

#### Getting Lost?

1. **F3 Coordinates**: Press F3 (Java Edition) to see coordinates
2. **Write Down Base Coords**: Always note your base location
3. **Follow the Bridges**: Bridges form tree patterns - backtrack to find previous intersections
4. **Build a Pillar**: If totally lost, build a tall pillar with torches on top as a landmark

#### Fast Travel

- **Ice Paths**: Place blue ice on bridges for fast boat travel
- **Elytra**: Endgame flying between spheres (requires End City)
- **Ender Pearls**: Emergency teleportation (risky over void!)

## The Story System

### What Are Story Blocks?

Story blocks are **suspicious sand or suspicious gravel blocks** placed at intervals along bridges. They contain pieces of lore about the Starry Trees world.

### How to Read Stories

1. **Find a Story Block**: Look for suspicious blocks on bridges (texture has visible brush marks)
2. **Sneak + Break**: Hold shift/sneak, then break the block
3. **Read**: Story text will appear in chat
4. **Continue**: Find more blocks to read the next parts

**Warning**: If you break a story block without sneaking, you'll get a warning message and the block won't break. This prevents accidental destruction of story content.

### Story Structure

The story is divided into **chapters** and **parts**:
- Each tree cluster has its own chapter
- Each bridge segment has the next part of that chapter
- Follow bridges in order to read stories sequentially

### The Starry Chronicles

The current story, "The Starry Chronicles" by BaicaiBear, contains 10 chapters:
1. The Awakening
2. The Bridge Builders
3. Whispers of the Void
4. The Amber Staff
5. Skyfall
6. Roots of Despair
7. Starlight's End
8. The New Dawn
9. Echoes of the Past
10. Eternal Canopy

Each chapter unfolds the lore of the floating world and its inhabitants.

## Survival Tips

### Starting Out

**First Day Priorities**:
1. Get wood from spawn sphere
2. Make tools immediately
3. Find a stone sphere before night
4. Build a small shelter on spawn sphere or nearby safe sphere
5. Don't venture far until you have stone tools

**First Week Goals**:
- Establish main base on a large, safe sphere
- Find water source
- Set up crop farms (wheat, carrots, potatoes)
- Locate animal spawning grass spheres for breeding
- Get iron armor
- Map out nearby sphere clusters

### Resource Management

**Critical Resources**:
- **Dirt**: Limited supply - don't waste on pillars
- **Wood**: Renewable from wood spheres - don't deplete them entirely
- **Water**: Find a water sphere and keep it safe
- **Food**: Set up farms early - no natural regeneration

**Renewable Resources**:
- Wood (from wood spheres if you leave some logs)
- Crops (once you have a farm)
- Animals (breeding on grass spheres)
- Stone (very abundant)

**Non-Renewable Resources**:
- Ores (mine carefully!)
- Dirt/Grass (don't waste)
- Special sphere types

### Food Sources

**Early Game**:
- Hunt animals on grass spheres
- Find and replant carrots/potatoes from dungeon chests
- Fishing (if you have water access)

**Mid Game**:
- Wheat farm → bread
- Animal breeding (cows, pigs, chickens, sheep)
- Potato/carrot farms

**Late Game**:
- Golden carrots (best food)
- Steak from cow farms
- Automated farms

### Mob Defense

**Spawn Prevention**:
- Light up ALL bridges and sphere surfaces near base
- Mobs spawn in darkness just like normal Minecraft
- Slabs on bridges prevent spawns

**Combat Tips**:
- Fighting near edges is risky - you might fall!
- Build walls around important areas
- Use bows for ranged combat (safer)
- Keep shield equipped

### Death Recovery

**When you die**:
- Items drop at death location
- You respawn at world spawn (unless you have a bed)
- **Problem**: Death location might be 1000+ blocks away

**Prevention**:
1. **Set Your Spawn**: Place bed at base and sleep in it
2. **Mark Dangerous Routes**: Note dangerous sphere locations
3. **Carry Ender Chest**: Store valuables in ender chest before risky ventures
4. **Bring Backup Gear**: Always have a spare set of tools

## Advanced Strategies

### Base Building

**Best Base Locations**:
- Large stone or grass sphere (size 20+ blocks)
- Central location in explored area
- Near water sphere
- Multiple bridge connections for easy travel

**Base Essentials**:
- Bed (set spawn)
- Storage (lots of chests!)
- Crafting area
- Smelting/cooking area
- Enchanting setup (once you have diamonds)
- Farm area (on grass sphere)
- Animal pens

**Multi-Base Strategy**:
- Main base in Starry Sky
- Mining outpost near ore-rich clusters
- Nether base near ancient debris area
- End base for End City farming

### Efficient Mining

**Sphere Mining**:
Unlike normal Minecraft, you can mine ENTIRE spheres efficiently:

1. **Identify Sphere Type**: Make sure it's worth mining
2. **Strip Mining**: Start at top, mine in spiral going down
3. **Full Clear**: Ore spheres are worth mining completely
4. **Leave Frame**: For wood spheres, leave outer shell for respawning

**Best Mining Targets**:
- Iron spheres (entire sphere is useful)
- Diamond spheres (mine everything!)
- Ancient debris spheres (jackpot!)
- Stone spheres (building materials)

### Bridge Building

**When to Build New Bridges**:
- Shortcut between visited areas
- Access to distant visible sphere
- Escape route backup

**Bridge Building Tips**:
1. **Check Distance**: Use F3 coordinates to estimate distance
2. **Bring Enough Blocks**: Calculate: distance × 4 blocks/meter
3. **Start High**: It's easier to go down than up
4. **Add Supports**: Every 20 blocks, build a pillar down (makes it look cool!)
5. **Light Immediately**: Place torches as you build

**Emergency Bridges**:
- Keep a stack of cobble in hotbar always
- Practice speed bridging (shift-place behind you)
- Crouch at edges to prevent falls

### Exploration Tactics

**Systematic Exploration**:
1. Pick a direction from base
2. Mark each sphere type found
3. Return and document (write coordinates of valuable spheres)
4. Repeat in new direction

**Treasure Hunting**:
- Eye of Ender to locate stronghold spheres
- Ocean explorer maps for ocean monuments (if available)
- Fly with elytra in grid pattern searching for rare spheres

**What to Look For**:
- Diamond spheres (blue-tinted stone)
- Emerald spheres (green-tinted stone)
- Ancient debris spheres (blackstone in Nether)
- End City spheres (End dimension)
- Stronghold spheres (stone brick visible)

### Farming Setup

**Crop Farming**:
- Find grass sphere near base
- Till 9×9 area around water source
- Plant wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot
- Light up completely
- Automate with villagers (if you have them)

**Animal Farming**:
- Grass spheres for animal spawns
- Build pens on grass sphere
- Breed continuously for food supply
- Keep 2+ of each type as backup

**Tree Farming**:
- Don't destroy entire wood spheres
- Plant saplings on dirt/grass spheres
- Bonemeal for fast growth

### Nether Strategies

**Preparation**:
- Full iron/diamond armor
- Fire resistance potions (brew before entering!)
- Bow with arrows (ghasts!)
- Building blocks for emergency bridges
- Golden armor piece (piglin trading)

**Nether Survival**:
- Watch for ghasts constantly
- Repair bridges they damage immediately
- Ancient debris spheres are worth risks
- Avoid soul sand valley biome spheres (most dangerous)

**Nether Resources**:
- Nether Fortress sphere → blazes → brewing
- Ancient debris → netherite gear
- Nether gold → easy gold
- Nether quartz → redstone components

### End Strategies

**Entering the End**:
- Need Eye of Ender × 12
- Locate stronghold sphere (use Eye of Ender)
- Activate End portal
- Bring good gear!

**End Survival**:
- Endermen everywhere
- Don't look at them (use pumpkin helmet?)
- End City spheres have best loot
- Ender chests for safe storage

**End Rewards**:
- Elytra (flying!)
- Shulker boxes (portable storage)
- Dragon egg (trophy)
- End City loot (best gear)

## Configuration

You can configure Starry Trees through the config menu (requires ModMenu + Cloth Config mods).

### In-Game Settings

**Portal Settings**:
- **Portal Frame Block**: Change from packed ice to any block
- **Portal Color**: Customize portal swirl color

**Dimension Settings**:
- **Nether Portals to Scary Sky**: Toggle whether nether portals lead to Scary Sky
- **End Portals to Scarcy Sky**: Toggle whether end portals lead to Scarcy Sky

**Visual Settings**:
- **Rainbow Skybox**: Fancy colorful sky instead of normal

Advanced users can edit config files directly in `.minecraft/config/` folder.

## FAQ

### Q: How do I get back to the normal Overworld?

**A:** Starry Trees is designed as a replacement for normal world generation. If you want both worlds:
1. Start in normal Overworld
2. Build a packed ice portal
3. Light it to visit Starry Sky
4. Return through same portal

### Q: Can I use this mod with other dimension mods?

**A:** Yes! Starry Trees adds three new dimensions and doesn't override vanilla dimensions (unless you want it to using the GlobalSpawn mod mentioned in configs).

### Q: What happens if I fall off?

**A:** You die and lose your items. They'll be in the void, unrecoverable. Always be careful near edges!

### Q: Are spheres infinite?

**A:** Each world has a fixed number of spheres (50,000 in Overworld, 10,000 in Nether) spread across a large area (20km diameter for Overworld). You're unlikely to explore them all!

### Q: Can I respawn spheres?

**A:** No, sphere resources are limited. However:
- Wood spheres can regrow if you leave some logs
- Animals breed on grass spheres
- Crops are renewable
- Most spheres are abundant enough

### Q: Do maps work?

**A:** Yes! Maps show spheres and bridges. Very useful for navigation.

### Q: Can I use mods like minimap/waypoints?

**A:** Yes! Mods like JourneyMap, Xaero's Minimap, etc. work great with Starry Trees.

### Q: Is there ore distribution like normal Minecraft?

**A:** No - ore is in dedicated ore spheres instead. You'll find iron spheres, diamond spheres, etc. This makes mining more of a treasure hunt!

### Q: Can villagers spawn?

**A:** Villagers don't naturally spawn in spheres currently. You might need to cure zombie villagers or transport them via portal from normal Overworld.

### Q: How do I find specific spheres?

**A:** 
- Visually identify by color/appearance
- Use `/starry_skies_locate <type>` command (requires permission)
- Systematic exploration
- Note coordinates of valuable spheres you find

### Q: What's the goal of the game?

**A:** Like Minecraft, you set your own goals! Common goals:
- Build an amazing base
- Collect all sphere types
- Find all story blocks
- Get full netherite armor
- Explore 1000+ spheres
- Beat the End (if dragon exists)
- Build a massive bridge network

### Q: Can I play on servers?

**A:** Yes! Server owners can install the mod server-side. All players need the mod installed to join.

### Q: Does this work with mods like Create, Tech Reborn, etc?

**A:** Yes! The `goodies/mod_compat_spheres` folder contains sphere definitions for many popular mods. Sphere types for modded blocks can be added.

### Q: My blueprint generation is slow. Why?

**A:** Initial generation happens once and generates 50,000 sphere positions using complex algorithms. Be patient - it only happens once per world! Future world loads are instant.

### Q: Can I delete blueprints to regenerate?

**A:** Yes! Delete files in `<world>/starrytrees/` folder and restart world. New random layout will generate. **Warning**: Existing builds will be in wrong locations!

### Q: Are there biomes?

**A:** Technically yes (three custom biomes), but they behave the same. Sphere types determine terrain, not traditional biomes.
